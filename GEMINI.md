# ChineseWorkSheet Gemini CLI Extension

Connects Gemini CLI to **ChineseWorkSheet** (`https://chineseworksheet.com/api/mcp`), providing 6 stateless MCP tools for:
- Vector Tian Zi Ge / Mi Zi Ge / Jiu Gong Ge printable grid geometry calculations
- Chinese Ministry of Education Hanzi stroke order rules & 8 calligraphy primitives
- HSK 3.0 & 2.0 vocabulary tier analysis
- Mandarin Pinyin tone diacritic / numeric tone conversions
- Kangxi 214 radicals decomposition
- Tailored handwriting curriculum & printable PDF packs

Website: https://chineseworksheet.com
